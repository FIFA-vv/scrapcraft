Our Story & Craft

Bringing Back the Warmth of Handwritten Keepsakes

Scrapcraft was born from a simple belief: digital greetings should feel as warm, personal, and tactile as a physical hand-pasted scrapbook.

Inspired by Pinterest Aesthetic & Cozy Papercraft

Standard social media posts and auto-generated messages often lack sentiment. Scrapcraft combines organic paper textures, washi tape accents, warm oatmeal tones, handwritten typography, and interactive secret candle wishes into a digital heirloom.

🕯️

Interactive Memories

Flickering flames that extinguish on click, releasing real celebratory confetti and gentle chime notes.

🎨

Curated Aesthetic

Harmonious color palettes (Soft Cream, Oatmeal, Espresso, Sage, Blush) with 9 wallpaper background patterns.

📦

Permanent Keepsakes

Every generated page stays live permanently and includes a standalone offline downloadable ZIP bundle.
